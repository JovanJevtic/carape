## PHOTOGRAPHY BRIEF TEMPLATE

Use this brief if commissioning custom photography:

### EN POINTE Brand Photography Brief

**Brand**: EN POINTE - Premium Women's Socks
**Aesthetic**: Editorial Chic, Fashion Magazine
**Mood**: Confident, Playful, Elegant, Slightly Cheeky

**Color Direction**:
- Primary: Deep black backgrounds (#0A0A0A)
- Secondary: Pure white accents (#FAFAFA)
- Accent: Hot Pink highlights (#FF007F)

**Lighting**:
- Dramatic, directional
- High contrast with deep shadows
- Subtle rim lighting on products
- Soft but defined on models

**Styling**:
- Minimal, intentional
- Focus on legs/feet/socks
- Modern, trendy accessories
- Clean, uncluttered frames

**Mood References**:
- Wolford campaigns
- Vogue editorial spreads
- Harper's Bazaar fashion stories
- Awwwards dark e-commerce sites

**Model Direction**:
- Confident, self-assured poses
- Playful but sophisticated
- Not overly sexualized
- Age range: 20-30

**Product Shots**:
- Clean, minimal backgrounds
- Show texture and quality
- Lifestyle context when appropriate
- Consistent lighting across range

## BUDGET CONSIDERATIONS

### Free Options (Limited Selection)
- Unsplash + Pexels for lifestyle backgrounds
- Generic fashion imagery (will need heavy curation)
- Estimated savings: 100%
- Risk: Generic look, limited sock-specific imagery

### Mid-Budget ($500-2,000)
- iStock/Adobe Stock subscription
- 50-100 premium images
- Good for: Product backgrounds, lifestyle, some editorial
- Risk: May still need custom product shots

### Premium Budget ($2,000-10,000)
- Getty Images editorial license
- Custom product photography session
- Professional model lifestyle shoot
- Result: Unique, on-brand imagery

### Ideal Investment
- Custom product photography: Essential (stock socks won't match products)
- Stock for lifestyle/backgrounds: Cost-effective
- Custom model shoot: 1-2 sessions for hero/lookbook
